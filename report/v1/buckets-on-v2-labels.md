# The withdrawn bucket scheme, on the v2 labels (record)

> **Withdrawn on 24 September 2026.** The independent audit ([review/critical-review-grok.md](../../review/critical-review-grok.md), claim 3) found that these buckets do not hold. "Cost-only" was the residual bin for every other measured card, and most of it has no comparison at all. None of the 91 "materially different" cards did something that was unavailable before: an LLM, rules, a vendor API or a classic model already did each job. Report v3 ([../landscape.md](../landscape.md)) replaces the buckets with a measurement ladder and the audited estimates. This page keeps the tables as report v2 published them (its section 8), regenerated by `scripts/buckets-reviewed.py` on the Sonnet v2 labels of the 5,709-post use-case base. Nothing in the current report reads these files.

**Deviation from pre-registration.** The positioning post fixed four tests before the labels existed. Read literally on the v2 labels, they place only 25.3% of cards: 4,264 (74.7%) fall into no bucket, because the tests leave out demos with no cost or latency claim, measured cards with no baseline, and the frame and feel tiers. So the scheme was finished after the data arrived. The first applied version filled the gaps by widening the rules: it let the frame and feel tiers and a rules baseline into "material" (219 feel-tier or frame-tier cards on v2 labels, 214 of them games), counted every unmeasured card that leads with capability as hype, and made cost-only the home of every other measured card. The corrected rules keep the pre-registered material test (turn or interaction tier, realtime, measured), because Jev's own p50 of about 400 ms is outside the frame and feel budgets; they add a rules baseline to it, move measured frame-tier and feel-tier cards to a separate "fast loop" bucket, report measured realtime results against a frontier LLM on their own, keep hype for unmeasured cards that claim a cost, speed or accuracy win or name a comparison, and call every other unmeasured card a demo.

Corrected material plus material vs frontier: **91 cards (1.6%)**, 88 of them outside games (1.5% of posts); 1 is measured production. By family: moderate 18, live 12, voice 12, browser 11, collab 8, classify 8, agent 7, trading 5, search 3, evals 3, games 3, data 1.
